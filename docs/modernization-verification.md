# Native-shell modernization — verification and handoff

Companion to the revised Liquid Glass / modernization plan. This is the Phase 5
runtime matrix and the Phase 6 handoff in one place. Every claim names the
environment it was proven on; an unavailable runtime is a named gap, not a pass.

## Window preference and restoration

- The launch *decision* is deterministic: `--windowed` overrides the
  preference; otherwise the Settings scene's **Open in full screen**
  (`openInFullScreen` defaults key, off by default) decides whether the
  launch asks for a Space.
- The saved window frame restores either way; the preference only governs
  fullscreen, and only at launch.
- Reopening the Library or a deep-link handoff never re-applies the preference.
- The decision's *outcome* is a correction attempt, not a guarantee: a
  fullscreen record left over from the kiosk era can still land the window
  on a Space if its restoration entry arrives after the correction's
  observers retire — reproduced once during this lane and tracked as #252.
- `Settings` is a separate scene; it is never a fallback target for window or
  deep-link routing.

## Observation scope

`AppModel` and its consumers migrated to `@Observable` (`@StateObject` →
`@State`, `.environment(model)` injection, bookkeeping under
`@ObservationIgnored`). Other `ObservableObject` types (`FolderStore` and the
security-sensitive internals) were deliberately left alone, per the plan's
"narrowly scoped" instruction.

## Deliberately deferred (confirmed absent)

Catalogue search and `__manoramaSearch`; toolbar photograph stepping; custom
`glassEffect`/`GlassEffectContainer` over web content on the Mac; icon
redesign; the SwiftUI `WebView`/`WebPage` bridge rewrite; a strict-concurrency
sweep; CocoaPods (SPM only); multi-window galleries.
