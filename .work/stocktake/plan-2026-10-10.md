## C. Strip presentation — keep honest pixels

The strip's own contract is "images sit back-to-back with zero gaps, one continuous canvas".
A short frame currently makes a hole in that canvas. Fix in two parts:

1. **Structural** — in strip mode a frame that cannot honestly fill the stage keeps the stage's
   full height and centres the photograph inside it, so the run stays continuous. Touches
   `app/islands/Viewer.tsx` where the frame's inline width/height are set (initial render and
   the `onLoad` heal path, ~line 2046). Horizontal geometry is unchanged (width is untouched),
   so drag/docking bounds are unaffected.
2. **Presentational** — mark the plate in the app's existing hairline vocabulary, and give its
   column the top of Rule 3's allowed near-black range so the vacated area reads as a mount
   rather than a void:
   - `outline: 1px solid rgba(var(--ink), .16); outline-offset: 0` on the photograph's box
     (no rounding, no shadow, no colour — Rule 3 forbids all three).
   - the plate's panel `#111111` against the neighbours' `#0a0a0a`.

Design rules that constrain this: Rule 3 ("no colored frames, drop shadows, or rounded corners
on photographs"; stage neutral near-black #0a0a0a–#111111), Rule 1 (nothing but the dot over
the stage — so no labels), Rule 6 (quiet type).
